# Scientific Status

**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Purpose:** Give an outside reviewer a one-page view of what this project currently knows, what is disputed, what is speculative, and what would count as progress.

## Bottom line

This repository is a **research program**, not a demonstrated unified theory.

Its central long-term question is whether scale recurrence, anomalous observations, coordinated network behavior, and observer-channel effects can be tested as parts of a larger nested regulatory architecture.

The current evidentiary position is:

- **Scale recurrence (S):** active but unresolved. An earlier Rank-1 analysis was invalidated because of a mathematical defect. Corrected reanalysis remains necessary.
- **Regulation (R):** framework/calibration stage. No higher-level regulatory system has been demonstrated.
- **Network topology (N):** new hypothesis family. Patrick Jackson's work is being treated as a source of candidate predictions, not as evidence that a network exists.
- **Anomalous observations (V):** active. VASCO/POSS-I is currently the strongest empirical target because it has published claims, public scientific dispute, and candidate data for replication.
- **Observer-channel modulation (O):** explicit but untested. The ECM analogy is a hypothesis generator for possible masking/steering effects on perception, memory, interpretation, or social propagation.
- **Cross-level influence (I):** speculative.
- **Cognition/agency/system consciousness (C):** downstream and parked until branch-specific evidence warrants activation.
- **Materials / C-002:** preregistration preserved but no longer on the critical path because provenance-qualified sample access/testing is unavailable. Sample-free data reproduction and method calibration can continue.

## Most important current scientific dispute

The POSS-I/VASCO branch now has genuine adversarial literature:

1. **Bruehl & Villarroel (2025), Scientific Reports** reported associations between POSS-I transient counts, atmospheric nuclear-test timing, and UAP-report timing. DOI: `10.1038/s41598-025-21620-3`.
2. **Watters et al. (2026), Publications of the Astronomical Society of Australia** challenged dataset definitions, feature validity, plate systematics, Earth-shadow results, linear-cluster interpretation, and the nuclear-test association. DOI: `10.1017/pasa.2026.10230`.
3. **Villarroel et al. (2026)** published an arXiv response disputing key parts of the critique and arguing that the critical analysis uses a heavily filtered, underpowered subset. arXiv: `2602.15171`.
4. **Doherty (2026)** posted an independent arXiv reanalysis reporting replication of the nuclear-window and Earth-shadow statistics on the original dataset. arXiv: `2604.00056`.
5. **Bruehl et al. (2026), Scientific Reports** used machine-learning artifact probabilities and reported that nuclear-window and Earth-shadow effects remained after artifact-probability filtering. DOI: `10.1038/s41598-026-66148-2`.

**Project position:** unresolved. The existence of peer-reviewed papers on both sides is not a reason to choose a side. It is a reason to design a preregistered adjudication that both sides can inspect before analysis.

## What is established vs not established

### Established about the project

- The hypothesis families are explicitly separated.
- Branches do not inherit evidence from one another.
- Null results, invalidated analyses, and blocked experiments are preserved.
- Vallée, Jackson, Lehto, and VASCO are not treated as authority-based proof of another branch.
- Observer-channel modulation and system consciousness are distinct hypotheses.

### Not established

This project has **not** established that:

- Cosmic Octaves is a real physical recurrence;
- Patrick Jackson's Sphere Network exists;
- VASCO transients are artificial objects, UAP, or spheres;
- Vallée's control-system interpretation is correct;
- human consciousness is being manipulated;
- a higher-level regulator or conscious system exists;
- anomalous materials have non-terrestrial origin.

## Near-term publication target

The strongest path toward conventional scientific legitimacy is a **neutral, preregistered adversarial replication of the POSS-I/VASCO claims**, designed to adjudicate between the published supportive and critical models.

Preferred form: a **Registered Report** or equivalent preregistered confirmatory study.

Scientific Reports currently accepts Registered Reports, including scientifically justified replication studies, with Stage 1 peer review of the question, methods, analysis pipeline, sampling/statistical plan, exclusions, controls, and outcome-neutral quality checks before confirmatory analysis.

## What would materially advance the project

A result should advance only the branch it actually tests. Examples:

- corrected Scale recurrence survives a frozen null model and holdout;
- VASCO effect survives an analysis that explicitly models plate quality, observation schedule, artifact probability, and dataset-definition uncertainty;
- Jackson-derived network prediction succeeds on untouched data against independent-event and survey-geometry alternatives;
- observer reports diverge from independent sensor channels in a replicated, directional way not explained by ordinary cognition/social models;
- a candidate disturbance is followed by a response that demonstrably moves a predeclared protected state toward a viability region, with feedback/recovery.
