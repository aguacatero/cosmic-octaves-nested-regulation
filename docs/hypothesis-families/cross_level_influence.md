# Cross-Level Influence (I)

**Status**: SPECULATIVE → TESTABLE → ACTIVE (only after I-model has independent support AND bridge promotion gates met).

## Sub-branches
- I0 — Confounding / ordinary shared causes (default; most parsimonious)
- I1 — Predictive macro→micro relationship (macro state predicts micro behavior beyond what micro alone predicts)
- I2a — Ordinary mediated macro→micro causation (known physics as mediator)
- I2b — Higher-level state has additional predictive/causal value after known lower-level mediators modeled (stronger claim; relevant to nested regulation)
- I3 — Regulatory higher→lower influence (strongest; requires Q10 regulatory evidence)

## Prerequisite
Does NOT require:
- Cosmic Octaves (S)
- UAP (V)
- Consciousness (C3)
- Agency (C2)

## Key Distinction
- I2a = ordinary causation (known physics explains it)
- I2b = non-ordinary; the higher level adds value beyond known mediators
- I3 = regulatory; higher level actively influences lower level (stronger than predictive)

## Bridge
B-RI (Regulation ↔ Influence) and B-IV (Influence ↔ Anomalous Manifestation) are SPECULATIVE. Both require:
- Both parent branches have ≥1 preregistered positive result
- Replication out-of-sample
- Novel joint prediction unavailable from either parent alone
- Bridge frozen before joint data inspected
- Combined model beats independent-parent model on held-out prediction
