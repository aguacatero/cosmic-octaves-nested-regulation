# D2 — Hypothesis Ladder H0–H6

Each layer requires evidence beyond the preceding layer. This file formalizes claim, predictions, required evidence, confounds, falsifiers, and relationship to alternatives. Falsification thresholds are written so any preregistered test can drive a binary outcome.

---

## H0 — Statistical / selection explanation

**Claim:** The apparent 10²⁴ recurrence arises from researcher degrees of freedom, sparse logarithmic sampling, flexible structure definitions, mixed measurement conventions, post-hoc selection, and/or generic properties of ordered multi-scale datasets. There is no underlying physical or organizational law.

**Predictions (if H0 true):**
- Pattern shrinks or disappears with independent holdout catalog (D, B in /wayfinder spec).
- Pattern shrinks or disappears with ordered/monotonic null models that preserve scale ordering (C).
- Pattern shrinks or disappears under specification-curve / multiverse over researcher degrees of freedom (D).
- Phase test (F) shows no non-random concentration of log₁₀(L) mod 24.
- All-pair difference test (G) shows no 24-dex excess in the full 105-pair spectrum.
- Negative-control datasets (e.g., ordinal position of cities by population; alphabetical length of chemical elements) produce equally "impressive" recurrences.

**Required evidence to rule out H0:** the spec's full statistical reanalysis (D4) — independent holdout + ordered nulls + multiverse + phase + all-pair + continuous statistics — must all favor the pattern before any extension is allowed.

**Major confounds:** small N (7 pairs), definitional ambiguity, threshold dependence, look-elsewhere, analyst's pre-observation of the pattern.

**Falsifiers (H0 gains credibility if):**
- Holdout catalog fails to reproduce ≥2 strong matches (deviation ≤0.2) under same protocol.
- Multiverse shows pattern is rare only in analyst-favorable specifications.
- Negative controls match or exceed pattern strength.
- Phase / all-pair tests show no non-random signal.

**Relationship to other layers:** H0 is the null against which H1–H6 are tested; if H0 holds, no extension is warranted.

## H1 — Real scale recurrence without consciousness

**Claim:** A genuine physical or organizational law produces the 10²⁴ pattern; no consciousness is required. Possible mechanisms include hierarchical stability windows, network scaling, energy/information constraints, symmetry/renormalization-like behavior, gravitational/EM/biological transitions, or generic properties of dissipative systems.

**Predictions:**
- Pattern survives H0 falsification tests.
- Pattern exhibits at least one cross-scale **dimensionless structural invariant** beyond length (modularity ratio, sensing/effector ratio, duty cycle, response-time/dynamical-time ratio).
- A missing-rung prediction (H) succeeds prospectively: a structure at the predicted scale is later identified.
- Pattern is at most weakly dependent on z (cosmological epoch).

**Required evidence:** D4 replication + cross-scale control-architecture comparison (D5/D7) + at least one prospective missing-rung confirmation.

**Major confounds:** pattern could arise from information-theoretic constraints (e.g., phase-space scaling) without implying consciousness; selection of "dimensionless invariants" is itself a researcher degree of freedom.

**Falsifiers:**
- Pattern fails to extend to a cross-scale invariant (i.e., length is the only shared feature).
- Missing-rung prediction fails prospectively after pre-specification.
- Pattern is strongly epoch-dependent in a way inconsistent with a structural law.

**Relationship to other layers:** H1 is the strongest non-anthropic claim; it is the natural alternative if H0 fails but H2 also fails.

## H2 — Recurrent control architecture

**Claim:** Structures at corresponding organizational scales share more than size — analogous control properties (sensing, integration, feedback, error correction, homeostasis, memory, adaptation, hierarchical signaling).

**Predictions:**
- Predefined dimensionless control-architecture measures (D5) are more similar between octave-paired structures than between randomly paired structures from the ladder.
- Effect persists in holdout catalog.
- Effect is stronger for "strong" pairs (deviation ≤0.2) than "weak" pairs.

**Required evidence:** D5 control-architecture framework applied to ladder + holdout; controlled comparison; effect-size statistics; pre-registered threshold.

**Major confounds:** "Control architecture" attributes are themselves constructs that may not transfer across scales (e.g., "memory" of a proton vs. "memory" of a galaxy); similarity criteria are researcher degrees of freedom; analogy ≠ mechanism.

**Falsifiers:**
- Octave-paired structures are NOT more control-architecture-similar than randomly paired structures under pre-registered similarity measures.
- Any "similarity" disappears under alternative measures.
- Pattern is driven by 1–2 structures and vanishes on leave-one-out.

**Relationship to other layers:** H2 is the most interesting claim under the spec's homeostatic analogy but requires careful measurement design. H1 + (failure of H2) is consistent with a non-conscious law. H2 is a **prerequisite** for H3 (emergent cognition) per the spec.

## H3 — Emergent higher-level cognition

**Claim:** A larger-scale system composed of humans and other subsystems possesses information integration, memory, adaptive behavior, or goal-directed regulation that could reasonably qualify as cognition. This does NOT yet imply subjective consciousness.

**Predictions:**
- A system-state measure (D5) of the human collective shows features typical of cognition: persistent state, learning, model-based prediction, context-sensitive response.
- Pre-registered tests show these features correlate with — or are predicted by — octave-paired lower-level system behavior.

**Required evidence:** Construct and validate a "cognition" measure for the human collective (without conceding consciousness) — e.g., IIT's Φ, integrated information, persistent state, learning curves; compare against a pre-registered threshold.

**Major confounds:** Almost any sufficiently rich stochastic system can be made to look "cognitive" under a flexible enough lens; "cognition" itself is contested.

**Falsifiers:**
- Collective state shows no information-integration, learning, or model-based prediction.
- "Cognition" scores do not exceed chance for randomly constructed nulls.
- Effect is fully explained by H1 (recurrent architecture without cognition).

**Relationship to other layers:** H3 is the lowest consciousness-adjacent claim; it stops short of subjective experience. A defended H3 is necessary but not sufficient for H4.

---

## H4 — Higher-level consciousness

**Claim:** The larger-scale system is not merely regulatory or cognitive but conscious in some meaningful sense.

**Required:** Explicit consciousness criteria; the spec demands distinguishing regulation / cognition / agency / consciousness. Candidates (must be operationalized):
- Flexible goal pursuit
- Context-sensitive response
- Persistent memory + learning
- Model-based prediction
- Novel information integration
- Adaptive communication
- Selective intervention
- Persistent preferences
- Counterfactual behavior

**Predictions:** Any operational measure of consciousness (e.g., from IIT Φ, GWT ignition signatures, predictive-processing evidence hierarchies) is elevated in the candidate higher-level system relative to pre-registered baselines.

**Required evidence:** Apply at least two independent consciousness frameworks (e.g., IIT + GWT) with operationalized measures; pre-register thresholds; show effect survives blinding and control conditions.

**Major confounds:** All current consciousness theories are contested; operationalization introduces researcher degrees of freedom; the candidate "system" (human collective) is itself a moving target. The spec explicitly warns: "Do not infer consciousness from regulation alone."

**Falsifiers:**
- Operationalized measures show no elevation above pre-registered baselines.
- Effect disappears under alternative operationalization.
- The same data are equally well predicted by H2/H3 (no consciousness required).

**Relationship to other layers:** H4 is a major claim. The spec is explicit that H4 requires evidence beyond H3.

---

## H5 — Higher-level causal influence on human consciousness

**Claim:** The larger-scale system can causally alter human perception, cognition, physiology, belief, or collective behavior.

**Predictions:**
- Pre-registered interventions or natural experiments in collective state produce measurable changes in individual human cognition/perception that exceed H0–H3 alternatives.
- Effect size exceeds plausible mediation by known social/psychological/physical mechanisms.

**Required evidence:** Causal-inference-grade designs — RCT, instrumental variables, natural experiments, or pre-registered time-series with state-space models. Cannot rely on correlation alone.

**Major confounds:** Confounding by media, culture, suggestion, demand characteristics, reverse causation; UAP-related effects in particular are subject to attention/media cycles.

**Falsifiers:**
- No causal effect in pre-registered tests.
- Effect is fully explained by media / social / psychological mediation.
- Effect does not exceed H1–H3 alternatives.

**Relationship to other layers:** H5 is necessary for H6 (Vallée-style manifestation). H5 does NOT require H3/H4 — a non-conscious regulator could causally influence humans.

---

## H6 — Vallée-style manifestation / control-system model

**Claim:** Some anomalous phenomena (potentially a subset of UAP-related events) function as one output channel of a higher-level control system and produce physical, psychological, physiological, informational, or cultural effects that participate in feedback regulation. (Vallée's hypothesis, formalized.)

**Predictions:** the full D6 / D5 predictive package:
- State-dependent triggering (D5.1): pre-registered collective-state index predicts manifestation rate.
- Corrective direction (D5.2): manifestations push the regulated variable toward a pre-specified range.
- Dose-response (D5.3): larger deviation → larger response.
- Latency (D5.4): reproducible τ_response = t_manifestation − t_disturbance.
- Feedback termination (D5.5): manifestations decline or shift as the variable returns to range.
- Adaptation (D5.6): if a manifestation no longer produces impact, the controller changes strategy.
- Memory (D5.7): hysteresis, path-dependence, repeated-location/witness effects.
- Overshoot/oscillation (D5.8): damped oscillation in time.

**Required evidence:** D6 prospective + retrospective study with preregistration, blinding, evidentiary grading, confound control.

**Major confounds (per spec):** random-event, measurement-error, reporting/attention, media-contagion, folklore/cultural adaptation, stress/misperception, deliberate human manipulation, unknown non-conscious natural phenomenon, conventional non-human technological intelligence, emergent collective-human dynamics, higher-level non-conscious regulator, higher-level conscious regulator — every one of which must be tested against.

**Falsifiers (15 named in spec):** no state-dependent triggering; no corrective direction; no dose-response; no termination; adaptation not robust to media controls; no stable latency; hidden-target experiments show no anomalous info transfer; objective physical evidence doesn't precede consciousness effects; conventional models predict as well; octave-paired structures share no unusual control architecture; scale recurrence fails holdout replication; result depends on arbitrary choices; negative controls match; higher-level models don't out-predict simpler alternatives.

**Relationship to other layers:** H6 sits on top of H1–H5. Falsification of H6 does NOT falsify every form of higher consciousness — only this proposed mechanism. Falsification of H1 falsifies the entire stack at its base.

## Hierarchy summary

```
H0  statistical / selection      ←— base; falsification here kills everything
H1  real scale recurrence         (length only)
H2  recurrent control architecture (cross-scale invariant)
H3  emergent cognition            (no subjective experience)
H4  higher-level consciousness    (operationalized measure required)
H5  causal influence on humans
H6  Vallée-style manifestation / control system
```

Each layer requires evidence beyond the preceding layer. The spec's guardrail is exact: do not collapse H1 + H4 + H6 into a single inference. The bridge matrix (D7) labels which evidence currently exists, which is inferred, which is missing, and which experiment closes each gap.
